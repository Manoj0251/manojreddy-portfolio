Building Resilient Cloud Infrastructure

I'm a Senior DevOps Developer based in McLean, Virginia, with over 11 years of experience transforming how organizations build, deploy, and scale their cloud infrastructure.

My expertise lies in designing automated, resilient systems that minimize latency and service disruptions while maximizing team productivity. I specialize in reducing operational "toil" through end-to-end automation, implementing comprehensive observability solutions, and leading cross-functional teams to deliver high-leverage platform services.

💡

"My approach combines deep technical expertise in cloud-native technologies with strategic thinking about system reliability, automation, and business impact. I believe great infrastructure is invisible to users but transforms how engineering teams deliver value."

Throughout my career, I've led critical infrastructure initiatives at Freddie Mac, where I migrated microservices from Oracle Cloud to AWS EKS and developed Kafka integration libraries. At OpenText, I architected Kubernetes deployments on GCP, implemented Istio service mesh, and reduced access provisioning time by 50%.

I'm passionate about DevSecOps practices, infrastructure as code, and building platforms that enable developers to ship faster with confidence. My work consistently focuses on automation, security, and operational excellence.
